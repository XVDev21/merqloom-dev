# Catalog media provenance

Sol created three original raster illustrations for Merqloom on November 22, 2026. Each image was drawn from project-authored polygon geometry and textured procedurally with Python and Pillow, then encoded as WebP at 960 × 720 pixels. They depict fictional fixtures at illustrative scale. The product pages disclose that the visuals are illustrations and reserve the image aspect ratio. No external source imagery, fonts, logos, generative model output, or third-party artwork went into the files. The illustrations are included under the repository’s [MIT license](../../LICENSE).

| File | Fixture | Accessible description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [tray-natural.webp](../../apps/storefront/public/catalog/tray-natural.webp) | MQ-DIRECT-TRAY | Illustration of a pale birch desk tray with a shallow raised rim |
| [tray-walnut.webp](../../apps/storefront/public/catalog/tray-walnut.webp) | MQ-DIRECT-TRAY-WALNUT | Illustration of a dark Walnut-finish desk tray with a shallow raised rim |
| [pouch-natural.webp](../../apps/storefront/public/catalog/pouch-natural.webp) | MQ-SUPPLIER-POUCH | Illustration of a natural canvas organizer pouch with a zip closure |

The variant SKU selects the matching authored tray finish. Native product and variant records supply the displayed specifications, price and stock.

Astra approved the MQ-102 image-count and motion exceptions for the two-product catalog. Catalog cards use the representative product illustration. A product detail page uses only a known variant’s illustration and reserves the same space for a neutral placeholder when no matching asset exists.
